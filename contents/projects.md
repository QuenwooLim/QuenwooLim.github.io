### Sun Yat-sen University Computer Science Course Notes

> September 2023 - Present

An open-source repository of study notes for Computer Science courses at Sun Yat-sen University, covering mathematical theory and specialized courses in artificial intelligence. The repository has earned 100+ [GitHub](https://github.com/QuenwooLim/CourseNotes) stars.

### Industry-Academia-Research Analytics

> May 2025 - May 2026

An information system for industry-academia-research outputs, using large language models to govern large-scale data on institutions, researchers, publications, patents, projects, and activities and to generate multi-source insights and profiles. It models relationships among projects with a knowledge graph, assesses technical directions using patent and publication features, and supports vector retrieval with institution and regional filters.

### Enterprise Service Large Language Model

> May 2025 - May 2026

An efficient multi-source retrieval and analysis system for matching technologies, R&D teams, and enterprises in industry-academia-research technology transfer. The project covered data governance, model training, algorithm development, and the full workflow from requirements analysis to deployment and delivery. The system has served more than 3,000 research institutions and enterprises.

### Classroom Educational Robot

> May 2026 - Present

An embodied classroom teacher prototype for real-world teaching, enabling autonomous movement, real-time interaction, personalized speech synthesis, body language, and teaching-aid manipulation. The project includes an educational large language model and automated courseware generation pipeline that adapts teaching strategies to learners, collects and structures teaching materials, and generates courseware and lesson plans. The educational robot has been deployed in classrooms at the Affiliated Primary School of Guangzhou University.
