### AudioIP: Joint Speaker Identity Profiling and Spoof Detection

**ICASSP 2027 · Under Review** · April 2026 - August 2026

Research on speaker identity analysis and spoof detection under voice-cloning attacks. The work introduces the VoicePrint open-set benchmark and a dual-branch model based on a shared WavLM-Base encoder, jointly addressing speaker profiling and speech spoof detection.

### Copycat: Imitating AI-Generated Videos via Prompt Reverse Engineering

**IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy 2027 · Under Review** · September 2026 - Present

Research on reconstructing long, structured prompts for 10-15-second AI-generated videos without access to model weights or generation parameters. The work combines multimodal prompt extraction, commercial text-to-video APIs, video similarity evaluation, and gated LLM-based prompt refinement.
